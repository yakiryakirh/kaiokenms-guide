Quests Worth Doing

A level-by-level quest checklist focused on fast EXP, efficient pre-collection, useful unlocks, valuable rewards and quest chains worth combining.

⚡ Original Guide • Created & Maintained by -Yakir-H
Information compiled from 2 different websites: MapleRoyals community quest guides Global Hidden Street quest database
MapleRoyals is used for the “worth doing” recommendations, routing ideas and community notes. Global Hidden Street is used for individual quest reference pages. KaiokenMS can still have custom EXP, requirements or rewards, so the in-game quest text remains the final authority.
KaiokenMS note: The quest names, objectives and base rewards in this draft are organized from classic v83 quest-guide material. KaiokenMS removes many old level caps and may change EXP, availability, bugs or rewards. Use the in-game quest text as the final authority. The goal here is to identify the quests most worth checking, not to assume another server's custom behavior.
Source method: every quest card now has its own unique reference URL. Recommendations and efficiency notes were compiled from MapleRoyals community guides, while quest-page references use Global Hidden Street / Hidden Street. No MapleRoyals links are embedded in the guide.

Maple Island / Job Training Quests
Lv. 1–10
Objective
Complete enough starter quests to reach your first job advancement, then continue with the training quests given by your job instructor.
Reward
Varies
Efficiency: Fast, local progression with almost no travel. If your instructor sends you after Stumps or Slimes, combine the objective with nearby item-collection quests.

Important Quest Chains

Why this exists: Some quests are valuable mainly because they unlock later quests. This tab keeps those chains separate from the raw EXP ranking so players do not confuse prerequisite value with immediate EXP.
Mr. Wetbottom → Rowen's Cursed Dolls
Mr. Wetbottom's Secret Book unlocks the later Rowen chain. Save Curse Eye Tails/Lupin Bananas and avoid selling prerequisite materials.
Sand Bandits → Deo
Complete Ariant's Sand Bandit progression, including The True Identity of Sand Bandits, to reach The Bounty Hunt / Deo payoff.
Mrs. Reade → Ludibrium utility quests
Request of Mrs. Reade 2 & 3 combines well with other Ludibrium/Eos Tower objectives.
Alarm Clock Chain
The Alarm Clock → Assembling the Alarm Clock → Delivering the Alarm Clock. Keep Table Clocks and Cogs.
Omega Sector Elimination Chain
Eliminating Grays → Mateon → Plateon → Mecateon → Chief Gray and related Omega quests. Batch these into one visit.
Muirhat / Golem Chain
START HERE: click the trash can next to Muirhat to begin the hidden letter prequest (called The Lost Letter / Half-written Letter in different forum posts). Then progress through Stone Golems → Dark Stone Golems → Mixed Stone Golems → Skeletons → Drakes → Tauromacis.
Alcaster / Book of Ancient
A long multi-area chain, but strategically important because of its classic unlock value.
Horntail Access Path
Wars of the Dragons is part of the classic Horntail-related progression path. Treat it as an unlock quest, not just EXP.
